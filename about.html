Our Editorial & Verification Standards

Founded with the mission of eliminating expired codes, broken redirects, and misleading coupons, RobotCoupons employs a dedicated verification methodology that combines automated API audits with human testing across major gaming and e-commerce platforms.

How does RobotCoupons verify discount codes?

Every promotional voucher listed in our catalog undergoes rigorous 24-hour verification checks combining automated API audits with human testing across official game stores.

Are vouchers on RobotCoupons free for all users?

How often is the coupon catalog refreshed?

Our verification engines scan and refresh all product offers every 24 hours, automatically removing expired coupons and refreshing reward codes.

Can users submit newly discovered promo codes?

Yes, shoppers and publishers are encouraged to submit newly discovered codes via our contact page for immediate editorial review and validation.
